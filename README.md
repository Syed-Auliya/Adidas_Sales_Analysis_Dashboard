Adidas Sales Performance Analysis

Business question: Where is Adidas losing revenue efficiency across regions, products, and retail partners — and where should sales/marketing focus to improve margins?

Overview

Analyzed 2 years (2020–2021) of Adidas sales transactions across US regions, retailers, and product categories to identify underperforming segments and margin drivers. Built an interactive Power BI dashboard for stakeholders to filter by sales channel and time period.

Dataset: Retailer-level transaction data — region, state, city, product category, units sold, price per unit, operating profit/margin, and sales method (in-store vs. online), covering 6 major retail partners (Foot Locker, West Gear, Sports Direct, Kohl's, Amazon, Walmart).

Key Findings
$899.9M total revenue at a 42.3% profit margin, averaging $45.22 per unit across 17.89M units sold
West region drove the highest sales ($269.68M), nearly double the East region ($0.26M) — a signal that regional strategy (or retailer distribution) is heavily skewed, not evenly executed
Men's Street Footwear was the top product line ($209M), with a clear gap down to Women's Athletic Footwear ($107M) — suggesting inventory/marketing investment isn't matched to category demand
West Gear ($243M) and Foot Locker ($220M) were the top-performing retail partners, while Walmart ($75M) lagged significantly — a candidate for a deeper retailer-performance review
Sales showed strong seasonality, peaking in July ($95M) and dipping in March ($57M) — useful for inventory and promotional planning
Business Recommendations
Investigate why East region sales are negligible ($0.26M) — likely a distribution gap rather than a demand gap, worth validating with retailer coverage data
Reallocate marketing/inventory toward Women's footwear lines, which underperform men's lines despite comparable margins
Review the Walmart partnership terms or in-store execution given its gap versus peer retailers with similar reach
Tools

Power BI, DAX (calculated measures for margin, average unit price, regional aggregation), Power Query (data cleaning/transformation)
